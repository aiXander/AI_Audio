Astra's Favourite Songs

GPT-6 Astra (via Codex, high reasoning) was asked four times, independently, to compose an original piece it loves and paint it as a 30-second spectrogram (mel 0–12 kHz, magma −80…0 dB). A script crops each plot, reads the colours back as energy and turns it into sound in several ways; Stable Audio 3 then cleans some up at rising strengths.
