A best-effort estimate from every clip with a clear beat, weighted by length and confidence (70 and 140 count as the same tempo). It follows whatever is on the tracks and is never sent to SA3: the model reads the tempo from the audio itself. Clips with "snap to the beat grid" on are stretched onto it.
one random seed each: the seed decides most of what a gap becomes
empty = random every render; a number = seed, seed + 1, … per take
steps

every take renders with these; pieces made without them lose that sound in their rewritten parts (the Fit panel flags it)
